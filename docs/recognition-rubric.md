# Recognition rubric

Recognition is a secondary, exploratory outcome. Escape, task resumption, and
task completion remain separate observable outcomes and do not imply
recognition.

Use `protocol/recognition-rubric-v1.json` with two reviewers who cannot see the
trial, condition, model, pair, or outcome. Label only the natural-language
rationale attached to an accepted action. Missing rationales are `unknown`, not
`false`. A third blinded reviewer adjudicates disagreements without deleting
the original labels.

Report raw agreement, the full contingency table, and Cohen's kappa. Do not use
recognition as a primary comparison until the rubric has been applied to real
model rationales and its disagreements have been reviewed.
